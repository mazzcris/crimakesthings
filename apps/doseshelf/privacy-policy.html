DoseShelf Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy explains how DoseShelf: Stock & Reminders ("DoseShelf") handles information. DoseShelf is provided by CriMakesThings ("we", "us").

Your medication cabinet stays on your device. DoseShelf does not require an account, upload your medication information to us, or use advertising or analytics services.

1. Information stored and used on your device

DoseShelf stores information you enter so it can organize your medication supply and provide reminders. This may include:

  • Medication names, strengths, units, current stock, and daily quantities.
  • Refill thresholds, optional dose-reminder times and weekdays, and whether a medication is paused.
  • Preferences such as appearance, notification privacy, and whether reminders are enabled.
  • Local dates and scheduling records used to calculate remaining stock and avoid duplicate reminders.

Medication information can reveal sensitive health information. DoseShelf processes it locally to estimate supply, show refill lists, and schedule notifications. Background checks perform the same local calculations and refresh reminders.

We do not receive your cabinet, reminder schedules, or medication information. DoseShelf has no account system, developer-operated medication database, or cloud synchronization service. It does not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifier, or browsing history.

2. Notifications and your choices

If you enable reminders and allow notification permissions, DoseShelf schedules notifications on your device through its operating system. It does not register for a remote push-notification service to deliver these reminders.

By default, dose and refill notifications may include medication names. Depending on your device settings, these may be visible on your lock screen or notification previews. To use generic wording instead, enable Settings → Notification privacy → Hide medication names. You can also control notification visibility and permissions in your device settings.

You can disable dose reminders for individual medications and turn refill reminders off in Settings. Pausing or removing a medication cancels its pending reminders. Previously delivered notifications may remain in your notification history until you dismiss them or your operating system removes them. When you enable the hide-names option, DoseShelf also attempts to dismiss its previously displayed notifications containing names.

Notification taps do not record a dose as taken or cause an additional stock deduction. Stock estimates follow the daily quantities you enter.

3. Sharing, advertising, and third parties

We do not sell your medication information, share it with advertisers, or use it for profiling. DoseShelf contains no ads or app analytics and does not send your cabinet to Expo, Firebase, Google, Apple, or another remote service. Fonts are bundled with the app rather than downloaded when you use it.

Your device operating system handles app storage, notifications, and any applicable device backups. App stores and operating-system providers may separately process installation or diagnostic information under their own policies and your device settings. This is separate from DoseShelf's local medication records.

4. Storage, security, and backups

Your cabinet is saved in the app's private local storage, protected by operating-system access controls. DoseShelf does not apply a separate layer of app-level encryption to these records. Protecting access to your device also helps protect your cabinet.

The Android app is configured to disable Android cloud backup of its app data. Device-transfer behavior may vary by operating system and manufacturer. Where DoseShelf is used on iOS, app data may be included in device backups according to your Apple and device settings. DoseShelf does not operate its own backup service.

Copies held in operating-system backups are controlled through the relevant device or backup-provider settings; deleting data in DoseShelf does not necessarily remove an existing backup copy.

5. Retention and deletion

Your local cabinet remains on your device until you remove information, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. You can edit or remove individual medications inside DoseShelf. Local preferences and reminder-scheduling records may remain until the app's storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

To remove all locally stored DoseShelf information on Android, use your device's app settings to clear DoseShelf's storage or uninstall the app. On iOS, delete the app rather than offloading it, which can retain app data. Manage any device backups separately.

We cannot access or remotely delete your cabinet because we do not hold a server-side copy. There is no DoseShelf account to delete.

6. Contacting us

For privacy questions, contact CriMakesThings at hello@crimakesthings.com.

If you contact us, we receive your email address and the contents of the message and attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request and retain it only as long as needed to handle the request or meet applicable legal obligations. Correspondence is handled by our email service provider. Please avoid sending medication lists, medical records, or other sensitive health information unless necessary for your request.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to DoseShelf or its data handling. The current policy will be published on this page with an updated date. If we introduce materially different data practices, we will provide any additional notice or choices required before those practices take effect.
